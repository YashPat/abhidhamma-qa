# Sheet accuracy status

Checked 2026-10-31 against Nārada, *A Manual of Abhidhamma* ch. I–V, IX ([budsas.org](https://budsas.org/ebud/abhisgho/abhis00.htm)), read verbatim with `scripts/fetch-text.sh`. Where Nārada is silent: Visuddhimagga (edhamma HTML) and the canon (tripitaka MCP). Each sheet had two independent checks; a claim was changed only where both agreed, and a final review checked the diff.

Every count in all four sheets matched. The errors were in definitions and in explanations of *why* a rule holds.

## What changed

- **citta.md**: ~55 claims confirmed. Fixed: bhūmi as sphere (not plane of existence); paṭigha is another name for dosa; great resultants lack abstinences and illimitables, functional lack abstinences; humans with deficient rebirth are an exception; mindless beings have no citta; immaterial states are jhānas; wholesome jhāna open to any non-arahant; fruition "even for a whole day". Cut: "unprompted is kammically stronger", diṭṭhi as "justifying conviction", fear under dosa, knowledge as "three characteristics", the reason given for the smile citta.
- **cetasika.md**: ~130 confirmed. Fixed: phassa's mark is touching; only mundane cetanā is kamma; diṭṭhi in 4 of 8 greed cittas; hiri/ottappa in every beautiful citta; doubt's eight objects (Vibh 17); anottappa and thīna similes replaced with Nārada's (moth, cock's feather); paññā "overcomes" ignorance. Cut: pig, bat and hot-coal similes, "paired with right view", "remorse" under passaddhi, "harder than compassion", the livelihood "narrows" remark.
- **sampayoga.md**: ~130 confirmed. Fixed: reason resultants lack illimitables (limited objects); kukkucca covers good left undone; perception is present in bare seeing; karuṇā/muditā 28 includes neutral cittas, with Nārada's "some say" dissent; sloth and torpor always together. Cut: most of the sheet's own theory (ekālambana "does most of the work", "one indivisible event", immaterial-realm base, why both methods are taught, 89-scheme "first-jhāna" claim, reasons for exclusions Nārada doesn't give).
- **vithi.md**: ~57 confirmed. Fixed: bhavaṅga's object; adverting/determining are functional, not "passive"; object enters after one or more moments; javanas 6–7 as a rule, 5 at death or faint; registration conditions (and the mind-door table); mind-door adverting only adverts; vodāna at later paths; death objects "presented by" kamma. Cut: Ledi Sayadaw seven-stage list, "receiving does no work", "the mind is somewhere it has never been".
- **sources.md**: Visuddhimagga page offsets corrected.
