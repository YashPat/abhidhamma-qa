# Sources beyond the canon

Where to read the commentaries, manuals, and scholarship that the MCP servers do not carry. Checked 2026-10-30. Read on demand; do not download these into the repo.

## Theravāda commentaries and manuals

| Work | What it answers | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Visuddhimagga* (Buddhaghosa), tr. Ñāṇamoli, *The Path of Purification* | The commentarial system as a whole: meditation, the aggregates (XIV), dependent arising (XVII), insight knowledges | [BPS 2011 PDF on Access to Insight](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/nanamoli/PathofPurification2011.pdf) (free, unaltered redistribution); [chapter pages on wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/book/visuddhimagga-the-pah-of-purification) |
| *Aṭṭhasālinī* (Buddhaghosa), tr. Pe Maung Tin, *The Expositor* (PTS 1920–21) | Commentary on the Dhammasaṅgaṇī: the characteristic, function, manifestation, and proximate cause of each factor; why prompting matters | [HathiTrust](https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/009013683) (vol. 2 full view) |
| *Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha*, Bodhi ed., *A Comprehensive Manual of Abhidhamma* | The standard modern guide; its explanatory guide carries the commentarial reasoning behind each count | Not free. [BPS](https://bps.lk/cover.php?id=bp304s); library ebook via OverDrive. Do not use the unofficial PDFs. |
| Pāli commentaries and sub-commentaries (aṭṭhakathā, ṭīkā), Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana edition | The Pāli original when no English exists (most Abhidhamma commentaries are untranslated) | [tipitaka.org](https://tipitaka.org) (VRI) |
| Nina van Gorkom, *Abhidhamma in Daily Life* | A teacher's explanation of citta and cetasika in lived terms | The publisher says it is free online; [buddho.org](https://buddho.org/book/abhidhamma-in-daily-life/) |
| Nyanatiloka, *Buddhist Dictionary* | Short doctrinal definitions with commentarial sources | [BPS](https://bps.lk/book/buddhist-dictionary); an online copy of the 4th edition is at [buddhismtoday.com](https://www.buddhismtoday.com/english/beg/dic1-titel.htm), status unconfirmed |

## Scholarship

| Work | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Noa Ronkin, "Abhidharma," *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* — [plato.stanford.edu/entries/abhidharma](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/abhidharma/) | Theravāda and Sarvāstivāda dhamma theory side by side, with a bibliography |
| `paper-search` MCP (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, CORE) | Journal articles. Prefer peer-reviewed work by established scholars (Ronkin, Heim, Bodhi, Karunadasa, Cousins, Gethin); flag self-published or AI-coauthored papers as such |
| *Journal of Buddhist Ethics*, *Contemporary Buddhism*, *Buddhist Studies Review* | Open or partly open journals that the search tools reach |

## Other traditions

For the Sarvāstivāda *Abhidharmakośa* and Yogācāra treatises, use `fojin` (CBETA T1558, T1585). For Tibetan Mahāyāna in English, 84000 ([read.84000.co](https://read.84000.co)) is readable with WebFetch.
